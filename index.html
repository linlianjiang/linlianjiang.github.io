Updated in October 2026.


I am a researcher at Mila and Concordia University (since Fall 2024), under the supervision of Prof. Yang Wang and Prof. Xinxin Zuo. Before that, I was a member of the Intelligent Content Learning (ICL) Lab at Jilin University, advised by Prof. Rui Ma.

My research focuses on generalization and adaptation in 3D systems across domains, sensors, representations, and environments, particularly under real-world deployment conditions, with applications in spatial intelligence, embodied AI and robotics, and autonomous driving.
